Cadence icons

Drawn for this app, not taken from an icon library. Round strokes on a 24 grid, with one flat tone per icon that fills only the part that is done or inside: the dose in the syringe, the elapsed part of today, the sample in the tube. Stroke is 1.75 at 22px and up, 2 at 20px and 2.25 at 16px.

Each tile shows 32px and 20px.
