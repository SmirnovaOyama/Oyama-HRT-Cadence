When a reminder is due

  • Taken logs it at the moment you tap. You can undo it next time you open the app.
  • Snooze 1 hour reminds you again.
  • Skip this one never counts as taken, and your curve shows the gap.

How reminders reach you

  • In the app

    On

    Works whenever Oyama Tracker is open.

  • When the app is closed

    Uses notifications on this phone. Our server keeps only the times of your next 14 days of reminders, with random labels. It never learns the medicine or the dose.

    What this means

    On iPhone, add Oyama Tracker to your Home Screen first.

  • Show the medicine name in notifications

    Off: notifications just say “Time for your dose”.

  • Add to my calendar

    Downloads a calendar file. Events are titled “Reminder”, so your calendar never sees the medicine.

  • Desktop app

    Reminds you while Oyama Tracker is running on your computer.

When you travel

Your schedules follow Istanbul time (UTC+3). If your phone changes time zone, we’ll ask whether daily tablets should follow local time. Injections always stay 7 days apart.
